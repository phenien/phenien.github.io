Information used by Sunday School Manager

Sunday School Manager uses account information and records entered by authorised users to manage Sunday School classes. Account information includes names, email addresses, user identifiers and assigned roles. Firebase Authentication handles sign-in.

Student records may contain names, dates of birth, contact details, parent or guardian details, photos, notes and last confession dates. The app also stores classes, teacher assignments, lessons and attendance. Religious information and children’s records can be sensitive: enter only what your church is authorised to hold and share records only with appropriate users.

Storage, access and use

Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore provide account and cloud data services. Records are used to display classes, track attendance, plan lessons, generate insights and reports, and synchronise changes. Data may be cached on the device for offline use. Notifications use the reminders and preferences you configure.

Administrators manage access; teachers and viewers access assigned classes according to their roles. PDF and CSV exports let authorised users create copies outside the app. You are responsible for protecting copies you export or share.

Google processes cloud data according to its service terms and Firebase privacy and security information. Cloud and advertising services may process data outside your country.

Advertising and tracking

The free version uses Google AdMob. Google’s advertising SDK may process device identifiers, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), ad interactions, diagnostics and performance information to provide advertising, reporting and fraud prevention.

On supported iOS versions, App Tracking Transparency controls permission to track activity across other companies’ apps and websites and access the advertising identifier. Declining tracking does not mean that all advertising data collection stops. Ad serving also depends on consent, region and Google’s policies.

You can manage tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. The optional Premium upgrade removes displayed ads. Apple shows the current price before purchase.

See Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures.

Purchases and third-party services

Apple processes in-app purchases through the App Store. The app checks purchase status to unlock Premium; it does not handle your payment card details. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Your choices and data retention

Authorised users can view, update and delete records within their permissions. Contact your church administrator or the support address below for account and associated-data deletion requests. Removing the app does not delete cloud records or other users’ exported copies. Cloud records remain until deleted through the service; provider backups may have separate retention.

The app is intended for adults managing Sunday School programmes. Adults entering information about children are responsible for obtaining appropriate authorisation and handling requests from parents or guardians.

Contact and policy updates

For support, access or deletion requests, or questions about data handling, contact Paul Henien at smassservice@gmail.com. If you contact support, the information you provide is used to respond to your request. Do not include confession notes or other sensitive records in a support email.
