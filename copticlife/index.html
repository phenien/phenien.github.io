About CopticLife

CopticLife is a mobile application dedicated to supporting the spiritual life of Coptic Orthodox Christians worldwide. We provide authentic resources rooted in the teachings of the Church Fathers and the rich tradition of the Coptic Orthodox Church.

Our mission is to make daily prayer, liturgical knowledge, and spiritual discipline accessible to everyone through beautiful, modern technology. Whether you're at home, traveling, or anywhere in the world, CopticLife helps you maintain your connection to the Church and your spiritual practice.

Key Principles:

  • Authentic Coptic Orthodox content
  • Beautiful, intuitive design
  • Privacy-focused (no accounts, data stays on your device)
  • Works completely offline
  • Free core features for everyone
  • Regular updates and improvements

System Requirements: iOS 15.0 or later, compatible with iPhone and iPad
