CopticLife Android Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026.

Your prayers and reading progress

The Android app stores personal prayers, prayer history, Bible reading progress, reminders, selected feast alerts, and reading preferences on your device. This version has no app account or cross-device sync. CopticLife does not upload this prayer or reading data to a developer server or send its contents in advertising requests. Data may be included in backups controlled by your Android device settings.

Advertising

The free app uses Google AdMob banner advertisements. Google's advertising SDK may collect and share your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device identifiers such as the advertising ID and app set ID, app/ad interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Google states that SDK data is encrypted in transit. Advertising data is handled under Google's Privacy Policy.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform presents privacy choices before ads can be requested. You can revisit those choices through More → Ad privacy choices when that option is available. Android's advertising settings also let you manage your advertising ID. Verified premium users do not see banner ads. Non-personalized or limited ads may still involve data processing.

Purchases

Google Play processes purchases. CopticLife checks purchase information to verify premium access and does not receive your payment-card details. Google's terms and privacy information apply to store transactions.

Notifications and widgets

Prayer, Bible, and feast reminders are scheduled locally. Notification permission is optional and can be changed in Android settings. The home-screen widget uses calendar information cached locally by the app.

Sharing and external links

When you choose Share App, Android lets you select another app to receive the shared text. Links to our website, this policy, and Google information open external pages with their own privacy practices.

Your controls

You can change reminder and language settings in the app. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the local app data; backups maintained by Android are managed separately in your device/account settings. The app cannot delete advertising information held by Google; Google's privacy controls apply to that information.

Audience

CopticLife is intended for all ages, including children. The Android app does not ask for a date of birth. All advertising requests receive child age treatment with a maximum G content rating. The app marks users as under the age of consent for Google’s consent platform and does not request Android advertising-ID permission. These settings disable personalized advertising; ads may still process limited information such as IP address, app/ad interactions and diagnostics. Parents can manage purchases and app permissions through Google Play and Android family controls.
