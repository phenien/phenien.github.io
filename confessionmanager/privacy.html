Privacy Policy

Records stored by Confession Manager

Confession Manager stores the penitent details, contact information, confession dates, notes and reminders you enter. Penitent records are encoded as JSON in local app preferences; reminders are saved in local app files. The app does not use Core Data for these records.

Biometric locking is available for access to the app. Device protections and biometric locking do not replace care when entering, exporting or sharing sensitive pastoral information.

iCloud and exported copies

When iCloud is available for the app, Confession Manager attempts to synchronize penitent and reminder records through your private CloudKit database. Supported settings also use Apple’s iCloud key-value storage. Synchronization can occur automatically when an iCloud account is available; it is not limited to a manual backup action.

The app supports plain JSON exports and encrypted exports. Plain exports contain readable records, and exported copies are outside the app’s access controls. Protect files you create and share. See Apple’s iCloud privacy information.

Advertising and tracking

The free version uses Google AdMob. Google’s advertising SDK may process device identifiers, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), ad interactions, diagnostics and performance information to provide advertising, reporting and fraud prevention.

On supported iOS versions, App Tracking Transparency controls permission to track activity across other companies’ apps and websites and access the advertising identifier. Declining tracking does not mean that all advertising data collection stops. Ad serving also depends on consent, region and Google’s policies.

You can manage tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. The optional Premium upgrade removes displayed ads. Apple shows the current price before purchase.

See Google’s Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures.

Purchases and third-party services

Apple processes in-app purchases through the App Store. The app checks purchase status to unlock Premium; it does not handle your payment card details. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Deletion and control

Use the app’s record-management controls to update or delete records. Deleting the app removes its local app data but does not necessarily remove iCloud records, backups or exported files. Manage cloud data in your Apple Account’s iCloud settings and delete exported copies separately.

Contact and policy updates

For support, access or deletion requests, or questions about data handling, contact Paul Henien at smassservice@gmail.com. If you contact support, the information you provide is used to respond to your request. Do not include confession notes or other sensitive records in a support email.
